Mkulima Seedlings management app — how to use it every day, starting today.

Programu ya Mkulima Seedlings — jinsi ya kuitumia kila siku, kuanzia leo.

We start fresh today. Old records stay in the old books. From today, every sale, booking, sowing and attendance goes into the app — on any phone or computer, everyone sees the same records.

1. Open the app

  1. Open shamba-sokoni.onrender.com in Chrome.
  2. Install it: Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app). It then opens like any other app.
  3. Tap your area: Loise, Sales — Managers or Sowing Team. Loise and Sales type their password. The Sowing Team opens with one tap, no password.
  4. Language: tap English | Kiswahili at the top.

Never share the Loise or Sales password with the sowing team or casual workers. On a shared phone, sign out when you finish.

2. Before the first day Loise

  1. Catalogue: 60 seedling varieties are already loaded. Check each price and correct it; add any missing variety, delete what you don't sell.
  2. Inventory: count what is in the nursery today and enter the quantities. This is the starting stock.
  3. Employees: add every staff member (name, phone, role, salary). Attendance and payroll use this list.
  4. Customers: add regular customers now, or let them be added as they buy.
  5. Bookings: enter any orders customers have already paid deposits for, with the deposit amount and pick-up date.
  6. Customers who still owe money from before: record one sale for each with the amount owed and payment = Credit, so the balance shows in the app.

3. Every day

Sales Record a sale

  1. Sales → Add Sales.
  2. Choose the customer (or type a new name and phone), add each item and quantity. The price comes from the catalogue; you may change it.
  3. Choose how they paid: Cash, M-Pesa, Bank, or Credit (pays later). Enter the amount paid.
  4. Save — stock goes down and a receipt is ready to print or share.

M-Pesa: tap Request M-Pesa payment, check the number and amount, Send request. The customer enters their PIN and the M-Pesa code fills in by itself. If they paid to the till themselves, type the code from their SMS (e.g. SJK4ABC123) into M-Pesa code.

A customer pays a debt: Sales → Order Book → find the sale → tap the 💵 pay button.

Sales Bookings

Customer orders now and picks up later: Bookings → enter variety, quantity, deposit and pick-up date → Save. When the seedlings are ready tap Ready; when the customer collects tap Picked up.

Sales Attendance — every morning

Attendance → for each worker tap Present, Leave or Absent.

Sowing Team Record sowing

  1. Open the app → Sowing Team (no password).
  2. Enter the date, variety, quantity, unit and the expected ready date. Save.
  3. When the seedlings are ready, tap Ready → Stock on that line and enter how many seedlings came out. They are added to the stock for sale.

Stock in and out

Inventory → + In for stock received, − Out for losses or damage. Sales take stock out by themselves — don't do it twice.

4. End of the day Sales Loise

  1. Reports → Daily summary (Z report).
  2. Count the cash. It must match the cash total. Check the M-Pesa total against the till/Paybill statement.
  3. If something doesn't match, find the sale today, not next week.

5. Every month Loise

  1. Settings → Backup (Excel + JSON). Two files download: an Excel file to read, and Mkulima_Full_<date>.json to restore from.
  2. Keep both safe: email them to mkulimaseedlings@gmail.com or save them to Google Drive.
  3. Pay salaries and staff loans from Employees → Staff loans & salary; record expenses in Accounting as they happen.

6. Internet

  • The app works without internet. Work goes on; it is sent when the network is back.
  • The top bar shows Synced 10:42 (all sent), Offline — 3 changes waiting, or Sync problem. Tap it to try again.
  • Open the app with internet at least once a day on every phone, so others see your work.
  • Don't clear Chrome's data or uninstall the app while changes are waiting.

7. When something goes wrong

  • Wrong entry: open it, edit or delete it. Every change is recorded with who made it.
  • Forgot the password: Loise changes it in Settings.
  • Phone lost: change the Loise and Sales passwords at once from another phone.
Tunaanza upya leo. Rekodi za zamani zinabaki kwenye vitabu vya zamani. Kuanzia leo, kila mauzo, booking, upandaji na mahudhurio yanaingizwa kwenye programu — kwa simu au kompyuta yoyote, kila mtu anaona rekodi zilezile.

1. Fungua programu

  1. Fungua shamba-sokoni.onrender.com kwenye Chrome.
  2. Isakinishe: menyu ya Chrome ⋮ → Add to Home screen (au Install app). Kisha inafunguka kama programu nyingine.
  3. Gusa sehemu yako: Loise, Sales — Managers au Sowing Team. Loise na Sales wanaandika nenosiri. Timu ya Kupanda inafungua kwa mguso mmoja, bila nenosiri.
  4. Lugha: gusa English | Kiswahili juu.

Usimpe mtu wa timu ya kupanda au kibarua nenosiri la Loise au Sales. Kwenye simu ya pamoja, toka (sign out) ukimaliza.

2. Kabla ya siku ya kwanza Loise

  1. Orodha ya Miche: aina 60 za miche zimeshawekwa. Kagua kila bei na uirekebishe; ongeza aina inayokosekana, futa usizouza.
  2. Stoka: hesabu kilichopo kitaluni leo na uingize idadi. Hii ndiyo stoka ya kuanzia.
  3. Wafanyakazi: ongeza kila mfanyakazi (jina, simu, kazi, mshahara). Mahudhurio na mishahara hutumia orodha hii.
  4. Wateja: ongeza wateja wa kawaida sasa, au waongezwe wanaponunua.
  5. Bookings: ingiza oda ambazo wateja wameshalipia amana, pamoja na kiasi cha amana na tarehe ya kuchukua.
  6. Wateja wanaodaiwa kutoka zamani: andika mauzo moja kwa kila mmoja yenye kiasi anachodaiwa na malipo = Deni, ili deni lionekane kwenye programu.

3. Kila siku

Sales Kuandika mauzo

  1. Mauzo → Ongeza Mauzo.
  2. Chagua mteja (au andika jina jipya na simu), ongeza kila bidhaa na idadi. Bei inatoka kwenye orodha; unaweza kuibadilisha.
  3. Chagua njia ya malipo: Pesa taslimu, M-Pesa, Benki, au Deni (atalipa baadaye). Ingiza kiasi kilicholipwa.
  4. Hifadhi — stoka inapungua na risiti iko tayari kuchapishwa au kutumwa.

M-Pesa: gusa Request M-Pesa payment, kagua namba na kiasi, Send request. Mteja anaingiza PIN na namba ya M-Pesa inajaa yenyewe. Kama amelipa mwenyewe kwenye till, andika namba kutoka kwenye SMS yake (mfano SJK4ABC123) kwenye M-Pesa code.

Mteja analipa deni: Mauzo → Kitabu cha Oda → tafuta mauzo → gusa kitufe cha 💵 kulipa.

Sales Bookings

Mteja anaagiza sasa na kuchukua baadaye: Bookings → ingiza aina, idadi, amana na tarehe ya kuchukua → Hifadhi. Miche ikiwa tayari gusa Iko tayari; mteja akichukua gusa Imechukuliwa.

Sales Mahudhurio — kila asubuhi

Mahudhurio → kwa kila mfanyakazi gusa Yupo, Likizo au Hayupo.

Sowing Team Kuandika upandaji

  1. Fungua programu → Sowing Team (bila nenosiri).
  2. Ingiza tarehe, aina, idadi, kipimo na tarehe inayotarajiwa kuwa tayari. Hifadhi.
  3. Miche ikiwa tayari, gusa Ready → Stock kwenye mstari huo na uingize idadi iliyo tayari. Inaongezwa kwenye stoka ya kuuza.

Stoka kuingia na kutoka

Stoka → + In kwa stoka iliyopokelewa, − Out kwa iliyopotea au kuharibika. Mauzo yanatoa stoka yenyewe — usiitoe mara mbili.

4. Mwisho wa siku Sales Loise

  1. Ripoti → Muhtasari wa Siku (Z).
  2. Hesabu pesa taslimu. Lazima ilingane na jumla ya cash. Linganisha jumla ya M-Pesa na taarifa ya till/Paybill.
  3. Kama kitu hakilingani, tafuta mauzo hayo leo, si wiki ijayo.

5. Kila mwezi Loise

  1. Mipangilio → Backup (Excel + JSON). Faili mbili zinapakuliwa: Excel ya kusoma, na Mkulima_Full_<tarehe>.json ya kurejesha.
  2. Zihifadhi salama: zitume kwa mkulimaseedlings@gmail.com au uziweke Google Drive.
  3. Lipa mishahara na mikopo ya wafanyakazi kupitia Wafanyakazi → Staff loans & salary; andika matumizi kwenye Uhasibu yanapotokea.

6. Intaneti

  • Programu inafanya kazi bila intaneti. Kazi inaendelea; inatumwa mtandao ukirudi.
  • Juu inaonyesha Synced 10:42 (yote yametumwa), Offline — 3 changes waiting, au Sync problem. Gusa ili ujaribu tena.
  • Fungua programu ukiwa na intaneti angalau mara moja kwa siku kwenye kila simu, ili wengine waone kazi yako.
  • Usifute data ya Chrome wala kuondoa programu wakati kuna mabadiliko yanayosubiri.

7. Kitu kikienda vibaya

  • Umekosea kuingiza: ifungue, irekebishe au uifute. Kila badiliko linaandikwa na nani amelifanya.
  • Umesahau nenosiri: Loise analibadilisha kwenye Mipangilio.
  • Simu imepotea: badilisha nenosiri la Loise na Sales mara moja kutoka simu nyingine.
